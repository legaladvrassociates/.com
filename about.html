About Us

A Legacy of Legal Excellence

ADVR Associates, a trusted law firm in New Delhi, we believe every legal challenge demands a strategic approach and an unwavering commitment to justice. As a full-service law firm, we provide comprehensive legal solutions to individuals and businesses across Delhi NCR.
Our team of experienced advocates in Delhi blends deep legal knowledge with innovative problem-solving — whether it's a complex corporate law dispute, cross-border arbitration, or a delicate family law matter, every case is handled with the utmost professionalism and care. With decades of combined experience, our law firm has built a reputation for ethical practice, clear communication, and results-driven representation across civil law, corporate law, family law, criminal law, insolvency law, and tax and customs law.
