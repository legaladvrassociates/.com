Arbitration Law

ADVR Associates provides strategic legal support in arbitration matters, advising clients on how to resolve commercial and other disputes thru an efficient and structured dispute-resolution process. Our team is committed to protecting the interests of our clients and providing sensible advice at all stages of the arbitration process.

We help clients with all aspects of arbitration including:

  • Commercial Arbitration: Legal assistance in disputes from business and commercial relations.

  • Construction Arbitration: Help with disputes related to construction projects, contracts and commercial issues.

  • International Arbitration: Advice and representation in international and cross-border disputes.

  • Arbitration Agreements: Help understanding and navigating arbitration clauses and agreements.

  • Arbitration Hearings: Representation and legal advice in arbitration proceedings.

  • Enforcement of Arbitral Awards:Assistance in matters concerning the enforcement of arbitration awards.

Why Select ADVR Associates for Arbitration?

Arbitration can involve complex legal procedures, contractual issues and significant commercial interests. ADVR Associates adopts a strategic stance on each matter to assist clients in comprehending their legal position and achieving a suitable resolution. Our team has arbitration experience and we specialize in providing professional, confidential and cost-effective legal support.

  • Commercial Arbitration
  • Construction Arbitration
  • International Arbitration
  • Arbitration Agreements
  • Arbitration Hearings
  • Enforcement of Awards

Our aim is to provide efficient, confidential and cost effective arbitration solutions, suited to the needs of the client. The firm's broader knowledge of business law, insolvency, taxation, civil law, and criminal law complements its arbitration skills, which can be helpful when a dispute includes more than one legal issue.

We offer expert legal advice to assist you comprehend the procedure, safeguard your interests, and strive toward a suitable conclusion if you are embroiled in a commercial, construction, or international arbitration dispute.
