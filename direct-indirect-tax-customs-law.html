Direct Indirect Tax and Customs Law Services at ADVR Associates:

ADVR Associates, a trusted tax law firm in New Delhi, provides legal assistance in direct and indirect tax as well as customs law, helping individuals and businesses navigate complex tax and customs requirements. Our team focuses on developing practical strategies to address legal challenges, support compliance, and minimise potential liabilities. We also provide legal representation in tax matters before the Supreme Court, High Courts, and Tax Tribunals — serving clients across Delhi NCR.

We help clients with all aspects of Direct Indirect Tax and Customs Law including:

  • Direct Tax Matters: Legal assistance with tax matters relating directly to an individual's or business's income, profits, or other taxable activities.

  • Indirect Tax Matters: Legal guidance on taxes imposed on transactions involving goods and services, helping businesses understand and manage their tax obligations.

  • Customs Law: Assistance with legal issues related to the import and export of goods, customs requirements, and related disputes.

  • Tax Compliance & Advisory: Guidance to help clients understand their tax obligations, maintain compliance, and develop appropriate strategies to minimise potential liabilities.

  • Tax Dispute Representation: Legal representation and assistance when clients face disputes or challenges relating to tax matters.

  • Representation Before Courts & Tax Tribunals ADVR Associates represents clients in tax matters before the Supreme Court, High Courts, and Tax Tribunals, providing legal assistance throughout the proceedings.
